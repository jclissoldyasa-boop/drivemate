Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

In short: DriveMate keeps your vehicle logbook. We collect only what's needed to run it, we don't sell your information or show ads, we don't use tracking or advertising cookies, and your GPS location never leaves your phone. You can download or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

DriveMate is provided by Yasa Yard Management ("we", "us"), based in Victoria, Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Contact us about privacy at j.clissoldyasa@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

InformationWhy we need it
Your email address and a password (stored only as a one-way hash), or the account ID from Google or Facebook if you sign in with themTo create your account, sign you in and keep your data separate from everyone else's.

If you use DriveMate without an account, your logbook stays on your device and we don't receive it.

Location

When you record a trip, DriveMate uses your phone's GPS to count kilometres. This happens on your device only. Your location and route are never sent to us or stored in your account — only the trip details listed above. In the Android app, location can be used in the background while a trip is in progress (and, if you turn on automatic trips, after the phone goes on a wireless charger). You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Receipt photos

Photos you take of receipts are saved to your phone's photo gallery (in the Android app) and aren't uploaded to us.

Sensitive information

We don't ask for sensitive information (such as health or racial or ethnic origin). Please don't include it in trip reasons, notes or bug reports.

3. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for marketing. We use these service providers to run DriveMate:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the app, our servers and the database where account data is stored.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the app's code and download page. If you send a bug report, your description and the technical details may be published as a public issue on GitHub so it can be fixed. Your reply email and account email are never included.
  • Google LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc., only if you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook. They tell us your account ID and email address. Their own privacy policies apply to your use of their sign-in.

We may also disclose information if required or authorised by Australian law.

4. Overseas storage

Our service providers are based in the United States and process data in data centres in Australia and overseas, including the United States. Before using them, we took reasonable steps to make sure they protect personal information consistently with the APPs, including through their published security and data-protection commitments.

5. How we protect it

  • All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and recovery codes are stored only as salted one-way hashes, and sign-in sessions as hashes, so they can't be read back.
  • Every request to your data is checked against your sign-in, so one account can never read or change another's. We test this regularly.
  • Repeated sign-in, reset and report attempts are rate limited.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), in line with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

DriveMate doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. To work, it stores some information on your device:

  • Your logbook and settings, so the app works offline.
  • A sign-in key, so you stay signed in.
  • A short-lived cookie (dm_oauth, lasting up to 10 minutes) only while you sign in with Google or Facebook, to keep that sign-in secure.

These are essential for the app to function. Clearing your browser or app data removes them (and any logbook data that isn't saved to an account).

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and logbook data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: removed after 6 months without use, or when you sign out.
  • Bug reports: up to 2 years. When you delete your account, your account email is removed from any reports you sent.
  • Sign-in attempt records (used to stop password guessing): about 1 day.

Tax records: the ATO generally expects you to keep logbook records for five years. DriveMate doesn't keep them for you after you delete them, so download a copy first (Report → Download logbook).

8. Access, correction and deletion

  • See and correct your logbook in the app at any time.
  • Download a copy: Report → Download logbook, or Setup → Download full backup / Save a DriveMate data file.
  • Delete your account and all its data: Setup → Account → Delete account. This is permanent and immediate.
  • For anything else, email us. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you signed in with Facebook: deleting your DriveMate account (Setup → Account → Delete account) removes everything we hold, including the link to your Facebook account. You can also remove DriveMate from Facebook under Settings → Apps and Websites, and email us to have your account deleted.

9. Children

DriveMate is intended for people aged 16 and over.

10. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, email us and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (1300 363 992).

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change, and let you know in the app before significant changes take effect.
