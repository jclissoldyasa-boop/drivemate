What it does

A logbook that keeps itself

The deduction is only as good as your records. DriveMate gets them right without you thinking about it.

Trips start themselves

Put the phone on the car's wireless charger, or connect to the car's Bluetooth, and a trip starts in the right vehicle.

GPS counts the km

Kilometres are filled in for you at the end of each trip. Check them against the odometer and save.

Swipe business or private

Swipe any trip sideways to switch it. Move a trip to another vehicle and the odometer follows.

Your best 12 weeks

DriveMate finds the 12-week stretch with your highest business use, and can set it as your logbook period.

Odometer reminders

A weekly nudge on the day and time you choose, for each car that's due a reading.

Running costs

Log fuel, servicing, rego and insurance as you pay. On Android, receipt photos go straight to your phone's Pictures folder.

Ready for your accountant

Download the logbook and a year summary as spreadsheet files, or copy the summary into an email.

Every vehicle

The ute, the van and the family car, each with its own odometer, logbook and picture.

Getting started

Set it up once, in about two minutes

  1. Add your car

    Name it, enter today's odometer, and link the car's Bluetooth or wireless charger if you like.

  2. Drive like normal

    Trips start and finish around you. Mark each one business or private, with a reason if you want one.

  3. Claim at tax time

    Open the Report tab to see both methods side by side, then send the logbook to your accountant.

The ATO logbook

Everything the ATO asks a logbook to show

To use the logbook method you need a logbook kept for at least 12 continuous weeks, plus odometer readings and your car expenses. DriveMate records each item as you go, so the logbook is finished when the 12 weeks are.

The ATO asks forDriveMate records it
When the logbook period starts and endsYes. You pick the start date, or use your best 12 weeks.
Odometer at the start and end of the periodYes. Taken from your trips and weekly readings.
Each work trip: date, start and end odometer, km and reasonYes. Every trip, with GPS km filled in.
Total km for the period, and business kmYes. Worked out for you.
Business-use percentageYes. Shown on the Report tab and in the export.
Receipts for car expensesYes. Each cost is logged. On Android the receipt photo is saved to your phone.
